The JSON corpus contains 2,378 synthetic cases, including dense change coverage across all five digest slots and combinations of competing defects. The remaining 20 cases independently cover sequential and concurrent ownership, ordinary immutable access, reflected surface, copy instructions and forbidden member names. Scan controls are reported separately. The accepted caller contract keeps arrays stable during a call. External trust, resource custody and future policy decisions are outside the managed oracle.

The mandatory frozen controls reproduced a working nominal case and detected the targeted fault for each of the 34 variants in both configurations. The 29 reviewer-selected ablations and faults independently yielded their required failure, with a successful valid structural control in each variant. Both scanners detected the extra direct and delegate edges at their precise uses in a disposable core.
