## Planned adversarial qualification

**Prerequisites and controls referenced in each row.** D is a deterministic byte/state test with a positive trace, injectable clock/transport and fd-ownership counters restricted to the test build. N is an unprivileged Linux x64 process/socket fixture with procfs, same user/PID/time namespaces, SO_PEERPIDFD, SCM_CREDENTIALS, kernel polling and real fork/exec/close. Q is a separately authorized disposable WSL2 x86-64 target with a real Qualified root-owned installation, full loader/runtime baseline and actual production binaries, no test hooks. Q includes N. L is the existing unqualified B2 lifecycle fixture (existing delegation when exercising retained domains). I is the accepted integrated B3 regression fixture and its actual delegation/lifecycle prerequisites. Production positives and real rejection probes must use an external driver or peer; instrumented hooks cannot qualify shipped bytes. B5 deterministic journal/clock controls remain available without native privileges.

P is successful qualified B2, exact trace in the design. PN is the isolated successful direct process/packet exchange without claiming installation trust. L+ and I+ are passing historical v1 lifecycle and B3 sequences at the tested revision. For every negative case first demonstrate its control on the same build/kernel and input, vary only the stated condition, and record both outcomes. In deterministic rows P denotes the modeled success. **F** below means terminal refusal at the indicated phase, zero C-qualified return, no subsequent positive transition, no domain/HELD/capability/RELEASE, stop once when applicable, and all transient fds reclaimed. If 12 already reached R, distinguish local Ready from final C qualification. A failed positive or unavailable prerequisite is a blocked gate.

Record type/direction/sequence/session labels, latched reason, peer PIDs and scoped pidfd observations, count/type/flags of fds, phase, monotonic events and deadlines, stop/reap/cleanup evidence. Keep secrets, raw handles, authentication data and payload-bearing authority out of logs. Capture resource inventories at baseline and after failure/reap. Cells specify additional evidence beyond that common trace.

## Qualification limits, preservation and decision

The accepted host observation is a WSL2 ext4 `/` owned by 0:0, mode 0777, with no qualified `/opt/gagamba` installation. Root-owned installation, full native runtime/loader closure, the socketpair-versus-connect peer checks on the target kernel and timely completion of all S2 checks under D remain open native gates. A writable ancestor must refuse. No changes to that host or installed trust state were needed for this review. Privileged installation, production activation, passive descriptor v2 and full pre-RELEASE generation enforcement remain separately gated.
