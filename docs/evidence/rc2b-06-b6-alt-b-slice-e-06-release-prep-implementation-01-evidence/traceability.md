After validation, schema, topology, steps, flags and requested release index are fixed by the accepted template. Independent per-projection checks enforce those relationships before agreement. The mutation variants use differing step contents, topology and flags to isolate those checks. Equality and expectation mutations retain a successful complete-projection control. Representation mutations retain successful structural preparation before the full byte oracle fails. Input-array snapshots are required even when callers keep their input stable. Within-call concurrent mutation is outside the contract, so output aliasing and overwrite supply the executable ownership controls while bounded input copies are inspected in source and IL.
