Judgment calls kept narrow and documented here for G1: (a) WP01 cleanup reports always resolve Unknown overall because only domain emptiness is observable and Confirmed requires all items; (b) transport failure transitions to Stopping with MayHaveExecuted and a NotStarted root; (c) pre-commit stop maps caller causes to Cancelled/CancelledBeforeRelease and setup causes to Failed/FailedToEstablish; (d) a stop that arrives after all completion criteria are met does not overwrite Completed; (e) the WP01 release frame is the 32-byte manifest digest, an explicitly WP01-only fake-transport shape, not the Section 8 128-byte layout; (f) `OwnedPrerequisites` encodes the four Section 6 Owned preconditions as one pure record; (g) observations overwrite within allowed states while terminal publication is single-shot immutable.
