Correction: the Q1 integration now uses terminationObserver.ExpectedIdentity (harness UUIDs + reaper pidfd + kernel fdinfo PID) for AuthorizedStopEvidence. The observer's new ExpectedIdentityComesFromOwnedPidFdBeforeObservation test proves the expected PID/pidfd/UUIDs come from the owned reaper and harness before the waitid result.
