## Fresh independent qualification

Establish and record reviewer independence before execution. The reviewer must have taken no part in implementation or producer verification. Pin the exact implementation commit and tree, verify the authorization and frozen artifact bytes, independently select and measure actual assemblies, and derive independent expected operands, tests and oracles. Run all applicable requirements and supplemental negatives, demonstrate omission sensitivity on disposable derivatives, repeat the regression and policy gates, and independently establish compiled production-unreachability. Record actual commands, case membership, artifacts, results, limits and outstanding obligations. Produce a P4 disposition scoped to that exact revision for separate owner acceptance.

## Stop conditions

Stop for unexplained repository or artifact discrepancies, a required edit outside the allowlist, a new dependency or visibility grant, a production caller or effect, or any authority promotion. Failed or missing mandatory verification, changed baseline membership or unexplained skips, failed policy or sensitivity controls, and unresolved material P4 findings block progression. A separate owner decision is required for scope changes. [Entry stop conditions](../rc2b-06-b6-alt-b-slice-e-05-entry-owner-02-evidence/bindings-and-boundary.md#authorization-stop-conditions)
