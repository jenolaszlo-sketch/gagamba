**C. ENTRY BLOCKED**

The accepted E-4 milestone is published and its mandatory GitHub CI jobs passed. The Slice E roadmap makes the E-5 fixed launcher and custody interface eligible for assessment. The accepted design gives the launcher, original controller and driver distinct roles, specifies their on-wire contracts and assigns critical custody invariants. A bounded implementation authorization still needs concrete executable and closure bindings, an agreed division of the protected acquisition and approval inputs, and a frozen custody interface that can be qualified while production paths continue to refuse.
