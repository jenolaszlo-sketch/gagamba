Existing qualification is not transferable to added waits/callers/resources. Required new evidence includes removal/bypass sensitivity controls with a valid positive fixture, lock-order and withdrawal race schedules, interrupted reads and partial delivery, cancellation at each transition, original reaper/cleanup ownership and conservative terminal outcomes. Protected-native observations require Slice N's exact host/candidate authorization. Operational refusal encodings may be chosen by implementation provided they preserve these semantics; D1 does not require an extra owner commission merely to choose codes. The resource/handoff and consumer decisions are real prerequisites; a code-label decision alone is not.
