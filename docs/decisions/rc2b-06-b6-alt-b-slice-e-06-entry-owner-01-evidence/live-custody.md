## Live GAVA1 and contextual parser requirements

Preserve dedicated AF_UNIX SOCK_SEQPACKET, CLOEXEC/nonblocking endpoints, 64-byte `GAVA` header, version1, exact sender role/type/length/directional sequence and original session/execution/lease in existing .NET GUID byte order. Payload limit is 128 bytes with exact per-type sizes below. SO_PASSCRED is armed before delivery; every record has exactly one size-12 SCM_CREDENTIALS from the actual pinned sender with expected UID/GID and no unexpected ancillary data. Fresh connected endpoints require kernel peer identity; inherited pairs require original spawn custody and actual per-record credentials. Truncation, trailing bytes, wrong role, sequence, credentials or rights is terminal; close every received owned FD on failure. No wrap, resync or reconnect. The accepted E-4 decoder's types4–7 expectations use sequence1 on their respective dedicated directional context exchanges; it is not a general live sequence allocator.

## Separately required native E-5 work

First authorize a native contract that completes the already selected artifact/build/packaging boundary, actual host/boot representations, owned safe handle types, nonforgeable construction after observations, borrowed/owned/duplicated resources, cancel/disposal/partial-send cleanup, protocol types 1–3 and live failure mapping. The [frozen launcher decision](../rc2b-06-b6-alt-b-slice-e-05-design-owner-01-evidence/launcher.md) reserves Linux x86-64 ELF on Linux x64/glibc, `native/gagamba-authority/`, and `eng/linux-m1/build-validation-authority-candidate.sh`. Preserve its exact six-argument CLI `--phase <pre|post> --catalog <catalogId> --expected-catalog-sha256 <Hk>`. It reserves dedicated `Gagamba.Validation.Controller` and `Gagamba.Validation.Driver` project carriers; concrete emitted artifacts/closure inventories still need binding. Do not reselect those carriers implicitly. Its E-6 input must carry independently retained exact selection and original resources, not a `ValidationCustodyModelSnapshot`, GUID label, enum `Consistent`, caller approval bit or serialized replacement. Define which original owner closes each resource on each failed transition; do not invent a new issuer or extend the accepted wire.

Then separately authorize native implementation, producer controls, fresh independent review and bounded component qualification on an authorized disposable host. Component evidence must cover real pins, credentials, drop, protected opens, seals, FD leak/wrong-rights controls, liveness and cleanup. It does not qualify a yet-unassembled E-6 system. E-6 may integrate source behind refusal only after the handoff contract and component checks exist; it may rely on live evidence only under separately authorized qualification with real A and exact candidate provenance. Slice N repeats the applicable evidence on the final integrated candidate. Rebuilding changes the candidate digest and invalidates transfer of binary-specific approvals.

## Sequencing cycles and resolution

The apparent cycle is E-5 needing E-6 administrative transactions/first integrity consumer, E-6 needing native E-5, E-7 supplying a member of E-6's atomic set, and Slice N needing a runnable exact integrated candidate before that candidate is exposed. Resolve with shared contract decisions and component development behind unconditional refusal; co-develop native E-5 and E-6 transactions against the accepted ownership split; co-assemble E-7 before closing the atomic candidate. Separately authorize exact-candidate disposable-host qualification, preserving public exposure refusal. Native qualification permission is not general exposure permission. The final binary/case receives its own acyclic V→W→A approval and fresh live invocation; qualification must never approve itself or assume S2/final acceptance in a pre case. See [milestone dependency matrix](roadmap.md). No authority boundary or numerical slice is bypassed.
