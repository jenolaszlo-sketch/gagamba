# Recovery / owner / controller ordering and dependency matrix

Governing sequence is [plan §3.5](../../plans/rc2b-06-b6-integrated-start-authority-impl-01.md), five numbered steps, and the accepted Alternative B validation-only B3 amendment bound in [provenance](provenance.md). The commission's ten points expand those five; they do not impose a different protocol. The early context ACK is GAVA context acknowledgment, not WriterReady. B2's stop/evidence topology never enters the B3 transaction/writer path.

**Observed current inversion:** `Lifecycle/M1RecoveryScope.cs:418` waits for WriterReady before sending DirectBootstrap at 435 and receiving DirectRendezvous at 439. `Lifecycle/OwnerPreparationTransaction.cs:53` enters RunTransaction, acquires writer at 122, sends WriterReady at 145 and only then accepts direct connection at 151–152. Those existing ordinary paths are not evidence of the required validation ordering. Simply moving O's check earlier would deadlock against R's wait. R/O/C must change together behind refusal; ordinary v1/v2 behavior stays governed by its original contract.

Failure convention for every row: terminal refusal for incomplete, duplicate, wrong-version, out-of-order, wrong-peer or uncertain progression; burn applicable invocation/action latch; no retry, reconnect, replacement actor or legacy downgrade. Original stop/reap/reconciliation continues under original absolute deadlines, independent of successful admission. Close only resources owned by the failing scope; retain stop/reap resources until their original duty ends; incomplete evidence remains Unknown.

## Progression protection and required evidence

Freeze an explicit per-actor state/expected-message table in the later integration contract, bound to original invocation/action/phase and inherited deadlines. Each admitted transition commits once; duplicates, gaps, future messages, changed IDs and partially completed transfers terminate positive progress. Do not infer a transition from an object existing, a send return, an fd number or a new process with the same PID. No authority reconstruction from journal or disk.

Every wait must poll/service existing stop/death/deadline signals, including protected-read and pre-writer direct-authentication waits; no blocking filesystem work under short stop latches. Late completions cannot revive a terminal session. One outstanding action and separate local issuance/consumption/release latches prevent repeats; a failed action burns its slot even if the caller lacks an effect observation.

Future producer and fresh independent reviewer must record event traces and first-effect counters for both B2 and integrated pre/post, normal and mixed old/new actor combinations, loss at every edge, partial-rights delivery and late WriterReady/StartAuthority. Check R intent→writer disposal→spawn independently from O direct context→writer; assert zero O writer effects before OR09 and zero B2 writer/domain/staging effects throughout. Exercise real processes/native custody only under separate authorization. No live transition was run here.
