## Independence, exact candidate and acceptance

Producer completion hands off a committed source/tree and exact binary/case inventory. A **fresh reviewer uninvolved in implementation and producer verification** independently reconstructs requirements, expected policy/correlation and caller graph, builds/selects artifacts, executes own adversarial cases, inspects raw results and cleanup, and records actual reviewer/task provenance. Reusing this assessment's author or producer evidence alone does not satisfy that gate. Separate owner acceptance follows on the exact integrated revision; any relevant source/binary change requires a new digest and reassessment/evidence sequence. No independent E-6 qualification was conducted here.

Slice N's separately authorized disposable host evidence binds source/tree, candidate hashes, host/boot/kernel/runtime/features/delegation, actual credentials/namespaces/pidfds, protected metadata, cutpoints, intent/capability/physical attempt/target-init-exec observations, stop deadlines, resource cleanup and durable finalization. Required missing/skipped/unknown cases block qualification. Qualification permission and subsequent exposure/publication/release decisions remain separate.
