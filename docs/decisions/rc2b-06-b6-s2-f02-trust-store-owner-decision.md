## What the administrator approves

A separately authorized administrator must obtain the independent review and acceptance for the actual installed digest and scope. Before provisioning, verify the canonical artifact-set SHA-256, the exact proposed record bytes and their SHA-256, the qualification ID, production profile/version, RID, scope, host, source commit/tree, evidence digest, review revision and limitations. Confirm that the required qualification gates passed for that installation. S2 source acceptance alone supplies no such host approval.

The administrator provisions `/etc/gagamba/accepted-qualifications/<qualificationId>.json` with exactly four fields. `qualificationId` names the accepted qualification, `artifactSetSha256` identifies the reviewed generation, `qualificationRecordSha256` commits to the entire proposed record, and `accepted` must be the JSON boolean `true`. **Scope is bound by the record hash.** The installed association check independently checks the hashed record’s scope and identity fields. The administrator must establish provenance before writing the approval. A candidate, timestamp or producer report cannot supply it. A root-controlled file represents the administrator’s decision within B6’s trusted-root model.

## Withdrawal and future revocation

**Withdrawing the administrative approval blocks subsequent publication using that approval.** Each qualification attempt, including a retry, must pass the approval check. The tool reads the approval once under the installation lock. For an ordered withdrawal, the administrator must serialize the change with qualification on that host, for example by quiescing publication and allowing an in-flight transaction to finish before withdrawal. Unlinking a file concurrently does not cancel a transaction that already validated its bytes.

**Withdrawal does not revoke an already published qualification.** Verification continues against the protected receipt and selected record under `/opt/gagamba`. A missing or changed selected record fails its normal checks. Withdrawal itself changes no admitted or HELD operation’s generation identity, original deadline, retained stopping capability or recovery authority.

Revocation of a published installation for **future admissions** requires a separately approved contract. It must distinguish blocking new admissions from preserving the identity of admitted operations, keep retained stop/recovery authority valid, refuse unsafe cleanup, preserve generations without automatic removal and retain conservative Unknown handling. This decision defines no general revocation operation.

## Conditions carried forward

HD-1 limits privileged installation to a separately authorized disposable environment. HD-2 retains the minimal passive descriptor. HD-3 limits experimental native scope to WSL2 x86-64. HD-4 retains immutable generations without automatic GC. HD-5 requires compatibility proof for invariant globalization. This policy decision qualifies neither the real privileged installation nor native runtime/loader closure. Full native Linux-host and Arm64 qualification remain open.
