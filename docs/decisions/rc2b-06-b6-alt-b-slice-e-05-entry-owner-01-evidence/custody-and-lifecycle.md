## Resource and concurrency checks

- Originals and copies have separate ownership. Borrow original handles under custody protection during send and keep stop/lifetime references. A successful rights send queues kernel duplicates. A failure preserves sender custody. A receiver collects all delivered descriptors, including on malformed, empty or truncated input, closes each invalid copy and publishes only validated, scoped ownership once. Queued unreceived copies end on receiver socket closure. Transfer into a valid recipient leaves exactly one owning close of that copy.
- Spawn-local descriptor allowlists and atomic close-on-exec prevent inherited copies outside the reviewed paths. A numeric fd or PID is never treated as identity or closed because packet data names it. Inherited sideband identity comes from real credentials and spawn custody, with fresh peer checks on connected endpoints. R may forward the prescribed inherited grant and L identity references to its original O only.
- Temporary sockets end after valid drain or failure. Scoped context and L pin persist through the original terminal lifetime. Originals must remain valid while borrowed or being independently checked. The custodian closes owned copies on failure without closing borrowed originals or disabling stopping. Real kernel closure, process exit/reap and privilege status require native evidence.
- The shared/exclusive authority and install lock order, one-response latch, B1/B2 linearization, per-direction valid-record progression, one action in flight and at most one owner physical write preserve distinct responsibilities. Original monotonic issue/admit/session and tighter bootstrap/operation/C3 bounds still govern; stop servicing continues while waiting. Separately approved live invocations are distinct. The accepted design imposes no global quota or durable spent ledger.
- The exact E-5 seam for resource result types, lifetime enforcement, diagnostic encoding and cleanup proof needs a pinned implementation contract. This ledger is the invariant and test basis for that decision. [Verification and P4](verification-and-p4.md)
