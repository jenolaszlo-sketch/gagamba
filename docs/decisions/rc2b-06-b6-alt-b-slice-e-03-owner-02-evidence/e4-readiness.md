# E-4 entry assessment readiness

**Ready to commission a separate E-4 entry owner assessment.** OWNER-02 closes the E-3 predecessor gate for its isolated managed scope. E-4 remains a separately gated refusal-only GAVA1 package, with its own entry decision, bounded implementation commission and independent qualification. [Slice E owner roadmap](../rc2b-06-b6-alt-b-slice-e-01-entry-owner-decision-01.md) · [Slice E review §§4, 7–9](../../reviews/rc2b-06-b6-alt-b-slice-e-entry-01.md)

| Topic | Input or required assessment |
|---|---|
| E-3 outputs | Use pinned R2, the adopted v3-pre contract and schema, independently qualified bootstrap and payload behavior, supplied-context comparisons, legacy refusals, compiled isolation and the baseline P4 inventory. Treat the codec's outputs as structural data. Verify exact identities in the new assessment. |
| Frozen GAVA1 wire and provenance | Bind the accepted source bytes and originating owner decisions, reconcile external provenance limits and freeze the authoritative header and wire in Git. Verify the direct-context exchange, roles, ordered messages and credentials requirements against accepted sources. Resolve any missing rule before entry. |
| Authenticated context and cross-format handoff | Review how verified INIT and direct context carry original capability and catalog identities, original session/execution/lease and authenticated predecessor state. Confirm E-4 handoff and E-6 recomputation ownership. |
| Refusal, authority and sequencing | Define exact authorized surfaces and fail-closed limits for mixed-version, missing, stale or mismatched context and downgrade. Any E-1 production consumer or authority conversion requires its separate decision and applicable F-02, P4 and dependency gates. |
| Integration and qualification gates | Plan pinned managed vectors, regressions, artifact/compiled isolation and RELEASE/Debug controls. Require a fresh exact-scope P4 authority and sink re-audit before a first protected effect. Retain F-R1/O-R1, O-R2, O-R3 and the observation-to-effect residual. Native/protected claims retain Slice N qualification. |
| E-2 and downstream allocations | Reconcile all 39 retained qualifications and 23 obligations, notably E2-R24–R27 and E-2 F-01. Leave protected K/M/F/S readers and exact case policy at their allocated E-4/E-5/E-6 and Slice N gates. Retain the E-5 launcher/custody and E-7 driver requirements and the E-6 complete atomic dependency set. |
| Outstanding decisions | Confirm GAVA1 header and message provenance, authenticated context and cross-format handoff ownership, exact surfaces and consumer policy, and any approval-chain or custody dependencies that prevent bounded entry. Composite Q-E1, P0 durability, BB-02 and `NATIVE-PENDING` remain open. |
