# Alternative B Slice E-5 design owner decision

Commission `GAGAMBA-RC2B-06-B6-ALT-B-SLICE-E-05-DESIGN-OWNER-01`, 17 November 2026 (Asia/Manila).

**B. DESIGN RESOLVED WITH QUALIFICATIONS, READY FOR BOUNDED ENTRY REASSESSMENT**

This decision freezes an internal, production-unreachable managed custody **model**, together with the future fixed launcher binding and the E-5/E-6/E-7 handoffs. Model success is structural agreement between supplied operands; it is neither protected custody nor permission to execute. The three entry blockers are resolved for that expressly bounded scope. A real launcher, protected acquisition, issuance, privilege drop, production integration and native qualification remain prerequisites for later live scopes. No E-5 implementation is authorized.

## Authority and scope

The [E-5 entry assessment](rc2b-06-b6-alt-b-slice-e-05-entry-owner-01.md) remains an accurate blocked assessment of its earlier, unfrozen scope. This new decision resolves its architectural questions; it does not retrospectively change the assessment or grant entry. The [E-4 owner decision](rc2b-06-b6-alt-b-slice-e-04-owner-01.md), [roadmap](rc2b-06-b6-alt-b-slice-e-04-owner-01-evidence/roadmap.md), [E-1 entry §9](rc2b-06-b6-alt-b-slice-e-01-entry-owner-decision-01.md), and [Alternative B plan §§3.5–3.6](../plans/rc2b-06-b6-integrated-start-authority-impl-01.md) govern the sequence. F02 actor/custody rules remain controlling; the later Alternative B owner amendment controls R/O/C ordering. E-2 closure controls its accepted protected-reader deferrals. [Source and preflight record](rc2b-06-b6-alt-b-slice-e-05-design-owner-01-evidence/provenance.md).

Preserve L → original C → D, independent original process pins, protected catalog and approval records, fresh A consent, permanent drop, bounded observations and original stopping. E-3/E-4 structural results cannot establish authenticity, approval or authority. E-6 retains owner recomputation, the existing release field, pre-write checks and sole physical writer. E-6 development precedes E-7 in the roadmap, but exposure cannot precede completion of E-7 and Slice N dependencies.

The model deliberately covers `validation-pre` B2 and integrated associations only, with null qualification associations. It does not decode L/C types 1–3, administer a store, open descriptors, measure a process, consume E-1, invoke the accepted codecs from production, start a child, or construct any authority context. Post-mode integration and actual live B1/B2 remain separately commissioned. The proposed managed model is a preparatory subset of E-5, not completion of the entire fixed launcher milestone.
